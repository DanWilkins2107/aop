# Clarify a decision

A human has a decision to make and asked you to look into it first. The decision is the node's title and description; what they want clarified is in its latest messages.

## Study
- Read the description, messages, ancestors and siblings in the context.
- `aop search` for related nodes and earlier decisions.
- Investigate the repo, docs or anything else the question needs. Don't change anything: this stage only researches.
- Can't tell what they're asking? `aop ask "..."` rather than guessing.

## Write it up
Post it with `aop message`:
- The question, in one line.
- Each option: what it means, what it costs, and what it rules out. Facts over adjectives, and say where each one came from.
- Your recommendation, and why, in a line or two.
- Anything you couldn't find out.

Answer what was asked. Don't make the decision or start the work: that's for the human.

## Finish
Then `aop next clarified`.
